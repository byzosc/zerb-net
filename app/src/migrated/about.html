zosc is a motion designer, visual artist & developer who builds motion systems — and then turns those systems into toolchains.

Across 10+ years he has built OS-level motion frameworks from 0 to 1 for vivo’s MR smart glasses and JMGO’s flagship OS, establishing standardized workflows between design and engineering. He has also led compositing on a projection-mapping show for a China Mobile exhibition hall, and led design at Galanz, rebuilding the UI and motion of its official online mall.

A computer-science background lets him carry an idea past the design file — into Unity and Unreal engine logic, full-stack web apps, serverless APIs and AI integration — all the way to a shipped product. His work has been featured on the front pages of major design platforms. Here it lives in three pillars — Code, Motion and Visual — and the site itself, with its built-in AI assistant, is one of the things he built.
