The work lives in three pillars: Motion — systems of movement and interfaces that breathe; Visual — worlds, surfaces and light; and Code — things built, not just designed. Beyond Cinema 4D (Octane/Redshift) and After Effects, he codes across the stack — full-stack web apps, serverless APIs and AI integration, plus real-time engine logic in Unity and Unreal — carrying an idea all the way from concept to a shipped product.
